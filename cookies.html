Cookies Policy

This policy explains how we use cookies and similar technologies to make your reading experience smoother and more relevant.

Last updated: April 15, 2025 Effective: April 15, 2025

Depth Meets Space ("we", "our", "us") operates the website www.depthmeetsspace.com (the "Site"). This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and your choices regarding their use. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which provides more detail on how we handle your personal data.

In short: We use minimal, privacy-respecting cookies. Essential cookies make the Site work. Optional analytics cookies help us understand what content resonates. You can control or delete cookies at any time via your browser settings.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide a better user experience, and give site owners information about how their site is being used.

Cookies can be "persistent" (remaining on your device until they expire or you delete them) or "session" cookies (deleted when you close your browser). They can also be "first-party" (set by us) or "third-party" (set by a service we use, such as an analytics provider).

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential functionality: To ensure the Site loads correctly, remembers your cookie preferences, and maintains basic security.
  • Analytics and performance: To understand how visitors interact with our content — which pages are most read, how long readers stay, and where they come from — so we can improve our editorial offerings. We use privacy-focused analytics that do not track you across other websites.
  • Preferences: To remember choices you make (such as language or font size) to provide a more tailored experience.

We do not use cookies for advertising, retargeting, or selling your data to third parties.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

The table below lists the specific cookies we use and their purpose. We keep our cookie usage as lean as possible.

Cookie Name Type Purpose Duration
dms_consent Essential (first-party) Stores your cookie consent preference so we don't ask again on every visit. 1 year
dms_session Essential (first-party) Maintains basic session state for site functionality. Session
_pk_id Analytics (first-party) Used by our privacy-friendly analytics (Matomo/Piwik) to distinguish unique visitors. Anonymized IP. 13 months
_pk_ses Analytics (first-party) Short-lived analytics cookie to track a visitor's session. 30 minutes
dms_prefs Preferences (first-party) Remembers your display preferences (e.g., dark mode, font size) if you customize the site. 6 months

4. Third-Party Cookies

We do not set third-party advertising cookies. However, some features of our Site may rely on third-party services that set their own cookies. These include:

  • Google Fonts: We use Google Fonts to display typography. Google may collect your IP address when serving fonts, but this is not used for tracking or advertising by us. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Unsplash: Some images are served from Unsplash. Unsplash may use cookies for image delivery and performance. See Unsplash's Privacy Policy.
  • Embedded content: If we embed content from social media or video platforms (e.g., YouTube, Vimeo), those platforms may set cookies. We encourage you to review their policies before interacting with embedded content.

We do not control these third-party cookies. Please refer to the respective third-party privacy policies for more information.

5. Your Cookie Choices

You have several options for managing cookies:

  • Browser settings: Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to refuse cookies, delete existing cookies, or alert you when a cookie is being set. Check your browser's "Help" or "Settings" section for instructions.
  • Opt-out of analytics: You can opt out of our privacy-friendly analytics by enabling "Do Not Track" in your browser or by using our cookie consent banner (when available).
  • Cookie consent tool: If you are in a region requiring consent (e.g., EEA, UK), you will see a cookie banner on your first visit where you can accept or decline non-essential cookies.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the Site. Essential cookies are necessary for the Site to work properly and cannot be disabled through our consent tool, but you can block them via your browser (though this may break some features).

6. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that sends a signal to websites indicating you do not wish to be tracked. We respect DNT signals: when DNT is enabled, we will not set analytics cookies. Essential cookies may still be set as they are necessary for the Site to function.

7. Changes to This Cookies Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. We will post the updated policy on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.

We write about the spaces that shape us.

Depth Meets Space is an independent journal for people who care about how interiors and architecture affect daily life — not just how they look in photographs.

Our mission

Most design media celebrates novelty. We are interested in depth: why a room feels calm, how a material ages, what a floor plan reveals about the way we live. Our goal is to publish writing that is useful, honest, and built to last — the kind of criticism and guidance you return to years later.

We cover interior design, architecture, materials, lighting, furniture, and space planning. We write for designers, architects, and thoughtful homeowners who want more than surface-level inspiration. Every article is researched, edited, and written with care.

“Good design writing does not tell you what to think. It gives you the tools to see more clearly.”

What we believe

Depth over speed

We publish fewer articles and take more time. Long-form essays, practical guides, and well-sourced criticism — not recycled listicles.

Honesty over hype

We do not accept payment for coverage. When we recommend a material, a method, or a product, it is because we believe it is genuinely worth your attention.

Clarity over jargon

Architecture and design can be intimidating. We write in plain language without dumbing down the ideas. Expertise should be accessible.

Longevity over trends

We are less interested in what is fashionable this season and more interested in what will still matter in ten years — in design and in writing.

Who we are

Depth Meets Space is edited by a small team of designers, architects, and writers based in Europe and North America. Our contributors have worked on residential and commercial projects, taught design studios, and written for publications including Domus, Dezeen, and The Architectural Review.

How we work

Every article goes through a rigorous editorial process: pitching, research, drafting, fact-checking, and multiple rounds of editing. We cite sources, credit photographers, and correct errors promptly. When we do not know something, we say so.

We are reader-supported. We do not run display advertising, and we do not accept sponsored content. Our only revenue comes from our optional newsletter membership and affiliate partnerships with independent retailers we genuinely recommend. This keeps our editorial independent.

Get in touch

We welcome pitches, feedback, and corrections. If you would like to write for us, please see our contact page for submission guidelines. For general inquiries, email hello@depthmeetsspace.com.
